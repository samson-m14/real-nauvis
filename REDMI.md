# Real Nauvis — Climate Zones

**Real Nauvis** introduces a custom climate model for Factorio 2.0 world generation.  
The planet is reshaped into natural climate bands with smooth transitions:

- 🌲 Green northern forests  
- 🍂 Mid-latitude transition zones  
- 🏜️ Dry southern deserts  
- 🎚️ Custom moisture and temperature gradients

This mod does not add new items or enemies — it focuses purely on terrain generation to make Nauvis feel more like a real planet.

## Features

- Smooth north–south biome transitions  
- Custom moisture and temperature expressions  
- A new map preset: **“Climate Zone”**  
- Fully compatible with vanilla resource generation
